# HTH Evidence Policy

## Preferred source hierarchy

1. Guidelines from recognized professional societies
2. Regulatory authorities and official product information
3. Systematic reviews and meta-analyses
4. Randomized controlled trials
5. High-quality observational studies
6. Professional consensus and expert statements
7. Manufacturer information
8. Case reports and anecdotal information

Position in the hierarchy does not replace critical appraisal. Relevance, methodology, population, recency, consistency, and conflicts of interest must also be considered. Manufacturer information is never automatically independent evidence.

## Evidence status

- **established:** consistent, high-quality evidence appropriate to the scoped claim;
- **moderate:** reasonably consistent evidence with relevant limitations;
- **limited:** sparse or methodologically constrained evidence;
- **unclear:** conflicting, indirect, or insufficiently interpretable evidence;
- **not sufficiently supported:** available material does not support the scoped claim.

Evidence status describes only the quality and consistency of available evidence. It is not a treatment recommendation. Traffic-light colors must not be used where they could imply approval, rejection, or treatment selection.

## Recording requirements

Record claim scope, source identifiers, retrieval date, evidence status, limitations, conflicts, reviewer, and module version. Contradictory findings remain visible.
