# HTH Correction Policy

## Reporting

Errors should be reported through a repository issue or another published maintainer channel with module, version, statement, and supporting source where possible. Reports must not include personal health data.

## Priority

Potential safety errors are triaged first. Maintainers may temporarily withdraw affected content before the full review is complete.

## Correction process

1. acknowledge and classify the report;
2. preserve the original version for traceability;
3. verify the cited source and affected claims;
4. correct, qualify, deprecate, or withdraw the content;
5. obtain the required editorial and technical review;
6. update module version, review date, and changelog;
7. publish a clear correction note when the impact is material.

Source corrections must update identifiers and every dependent claim. Outdated modules are marked `deprecated` and excluded from current output when continued display could mislead.

## Transparency

Correction notes state what changed, why, when, and which versions were affected without exposing reporter identities or private information.
