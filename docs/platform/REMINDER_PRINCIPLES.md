# Reminder Principles

HTP reminders are a future optional documentation aid, not a treatment or decision system.

## Binding rules

- A reminder may refer only to an entry created by the user.
- HTP provides no predefined treatment plans or fixed product-to-weekday schedules.
- Examples and mock-ups are labelled as non-medical demonstrations of functionality.
- Entries and reminders are fully editable and deletable by the user.
- Reminders are optional, pausable, and disableable globally and individually.
- Notification text is neutral and configurable; sensitive detail can be hidden on lock screens.
- Skipping, pausing, or dismissing a reminder is not labelled a treatment error.
- Statistics measure documentation activity, not adherence, clinical benefit, or medical success.
- The platform does not decide whether an activity should start, stop, continue, or change.

## Example-label requirement

Any sample schedule must display a notice equivalent to: **Fictional interface example — not a medical recommendation or treatment plan.** Sample entries must not use presentation patterns that imply endorsement or a generally valid routine.
