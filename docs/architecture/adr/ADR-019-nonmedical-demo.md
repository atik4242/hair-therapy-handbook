# ADR-019: No medical content in the first demonstration

- Status: Accepted
- Context: HTH-008 validates structure and presentation, not clinical editorial quality.
- Decision: Demonstrate only how to read a module; include no diagnosis, therapy, product, dose, or schedule.
- Consequences: The renderer can be tested safely but does not prove readiness for medical publication.
- Open points: Qualified review governance must be operational before medical modules.
