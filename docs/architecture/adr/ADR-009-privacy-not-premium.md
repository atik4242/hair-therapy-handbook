# ADR-009: Privacy is not a premium feature

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Context:** Charging for baseline privacy, export, or deletion undermines meaningful user control.
- **Decision:** Privacy protections, access to one's data, export, and deletion are not restricted to a paid tier or deliberately obstructed.
- **Consequences:** Product packaging must not create weaker privacy defaults for non-paying users.
- **Open points:** Reasonable operational limits and abuse prevention require transparent, non-discriminatory design.
