# Knowledge Module Standard

Public modules use a traceable structure: summary; topic; investigated or used context; evidence; uncertainty; safety and limitations; common misunderstandings; sources; editorial review; and version/correction history. A section may be omitted only when its absence or non-applicability is explicit and reviewable.

Evidence and uncertainty are separate. Neither creates an individual conclusion. Modules inform; they do not diagnose, recommend therapy, or replace qualified assessment.

HTH-008 includes only the non-medical module “Understanding an HTP Knowledge Module” in German, English, and Turkish. It demonstrates structure and editorial metadata, not medical content quality.

## Compatibility gate

- No diagnosis: the demo describes a document format only.
- No therapy recommendation: it contains no treatment, product, dose, or schedule.
- No individual decision: evidence labels carry an explicit boundary.
- No health advice: visible safety text states the demo's limit.
- Future medically relevant content requires qualified evidence, subject, language, and editorial review.
- No personal data is collected or represented.
- No telemetry is needed because the public page is static and requires no behavioural measurement.
