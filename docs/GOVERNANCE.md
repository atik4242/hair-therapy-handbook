# HTH Governance

## Roles

- **Maintainer:** protects project scope, repositories, releases, and final merge decisions.
- **Technical Editor:** reviews architecture, builds, schemas, accessibility, and reproducibility.
- **Content Editor:** enforces neutral language, structure, traceability, and editorial consistency.
- **Scientific Reviewer:** optionally assesses scientific interpretation within their stated competence. This role is not automatically available and must never be implied.
- **Contributor:** proposes scoped changes and supplies required evidence and disclosures.

No role title implies medical licensure, institutional endorsement, or guaranteed specialist review.

## Decisions and review duties

Decisions seek documented consensus. Maintainers resolve remaining scope or release questions after technical and editorial review. Medically relevant content requires source review and, where available, competent scientific review. Lack of such review must be disclosed.

## Disagreement

Competing interpretations are recorded with sources, uncertainty, and the reason for the final editorial treatment. Authority is not established by seniority, volume, or commercial influence.

## Corrections, safety, and outdated content

Corrections follow the [Correction Policy](CORRECTION_POLICY.md). Potential safety issues receive priority review and may trigger immediate withdrawal or a prominent caution. Outdated modules are labelled, deprecated, or removed from generated publications until reviewed.

## Conflicts of interest

Contributors and reviewers disclose relevant financial, professional, or personal interests. A conflicted person should not be the sole approver of affected content.

## Manufacturers and sponsorship

Manufacturer requests and sponsorship offers do not receive editorial privilege. Funding or material support must be disclosed. Content independence takes priority over commercial interests, and paid placement, rankings, or guaranteed coverage are prohibited.

## Policy relationships

Governance applies the [Project Charter](PROJECT_CHARTER.md), [Editorial Principles](../EDITORIAL_PRINCIPLES.md), [Evidence Policy](EVIDENCE_POLICY.md), and [Product Admission Policy](PRODUCT_ADMISSION_POLICY.md).
