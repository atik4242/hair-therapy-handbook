# HTH Product Admission Policy

A product or procedure may enter the module library only when:

- it is unambiguously identifiable;
- purpose and category are clear;
- relevant sources have been reviewed;
- authorization status and intended scope are documented without extrapolation;
- known warnings are documented from traceable sources;
- potential interaction or precaution rules have been assessed;
- manufacturer statements are separated from independent evidence;
- editorial review has occurred;
- a version and review date are assigned.

Admission does not mean endorsement, recommendation, effectiveness, suitability, or safety for an individual.

## Status values

- `proposed`
- `under-review`
- `accepted`
- `accepted-with-limited-evidence`
- `deprecated`
- `rejected`

Each status requires a dated rationale. Accepted status permits neutral inclusion only.
