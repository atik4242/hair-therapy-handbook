# HTH Editorial Principles

These principles are binding for all shared HTH content.

1. **Neutrality.** Describe information without steering readers toward a product, procedure, or treatment choice.
2. **Evidence before opinion.** Prefer verifiable evidence over intuition, popularity, testimonials, or editorial preference.
3. **No therapy recommendations.** Do not recommend, prescribe, rank, optimize, or select therapies for individuals or groups.
4. **No diagnoses.** Do not infer, confirm, or exclude diagnoses.
5. **Sources required.** Every medically or scientifically relevant factual statement requires an appropriate, reviewable source.
6. **Traceability.** Claims must be attributable to a source, module version, and editorial change.
7. **Versioning.** Every relevant change must receive a recorded version or revision reference.
8. **Facts and personal records remain separate.** Personal observations are documentation, not general evidence.
9. **Uncertainty is visible.** State limitations, gaps, conflicts, and unresolved questions plainly.
10. **Respect for professional authorities.** Represent medical societies, regulators, official information, and treating professionals accurately without implying their endorsement.
11. **Openness and correctability.** Content must be reviewable, challengeable, and correctable through a transparent process.

## Prohibited editorial practices

- advertising language or promotional framing;
- unsupported healing or efficacy promises;
- product star ratings, rankings, or purchase recommendations;
- presenting manufacturer statements as independent evidence;
- presenting anecdotes or individual reports as scientific evidence;
- hiding contradictory evidence;
- replacing missing evidence with speculation.

Manufacturer information must be labelled as such. Contradictory evidence must be displayed fairly, and insufficient evidence must remain explicitly insufficient.
