# Contributing

Contributions must follow the [Project Charter](docs/PROJECT_CHARTER.md), [Editorial Principles](EDITORIAL_PRINCIPLES.md), [Governance](docs/GOVERNANCE.md), and [Evidence Policy](docs/EVIDENCE_POLICY.md).

## Required conditions

- Factual medical or scientific statements need appropriate, verifiable sources.
- Claims must be traceable to source identifiers and module versions.
- Do not add therapy recommendations, diagnoses, advertising, rankings, or purchase advice.
- Do not copy manufacturer text uncritically; label and separate manufacturer statements from independent evidence.
- Mark uncertainty, limitations, conflicting evidence, and missing evidence explicitly.
- Never commit personal data, health data, private photos, credentials, or proprietary fonts.
- Medically relevant changes require editorial review and, where available, competent scientific review. Do not imply that specialist review exists when it does not.
- New products or procedures require structured review under the [Product Admission Policy](docs/PRODUCT_ADMISSION_POLICY.md).
- Keep layout logic reusable and visually verify every generated PDF.

## Review workflow

1. Proposal
2. Scope review
3. Source research
4. Evidence assessment
5. Neutral draft
6. Editorial review
7. Scientific review, where available
8. Approval
9. Versioning
10. Publication

Each pull request must document completed stages, unresolved limitations, conflicts of interest, and the required reviewers. Corrections follow the [Correction Policy](docs/CORRECTION_POLICY.md).
